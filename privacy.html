Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

ClipStudio is a personal desktop application that runs on its owner's own computer. This policy explains what data it accesses through Google and YouTube, and what it does with that data.

What data ClipStudio accesses

When the owner connects a YouTube channel, ClipStudio asks Google for permission to:

  • Upload videos to that YouTube channel (youtube.upload).
  • Read basic channel information: the channel ID, name and profile picture (youtube.readonly).

ClipStudio does not access email, contacts, viewing history, comments, analytics or any other account data.

How the data is used

  • The channel name and picture are shown in the app so the owner knows which channel is connected.
  • The upload permission is only used when the owner chooses to upload a specific clip.

Storage and sharing

  • Sign-in tokens and channel information are stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • No data is sent to any server other than Google's/YouTube's own APIs.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Removing access

A connected channel can be disconnected in the app's settings, which deletes its stored token from the computer. Access can also be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google and YouTube

ClipStudio uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
