Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

ClipStudio is a personal desktop application that runs on its owner's own computer. This policy explains what data it accesses through Google/YouTube and TikTok, and what it does with that data.

What data ClipStudio accesses

When the owner connects a YouTube channel, ClipStudio asks Google for permission to:

  • Upload videos to that YouTube channel (youtube.upload).
  • Read basic channel information: the channel ID, name and profile picture (youtube.readonly).

When the owner connects a TikTok account, ClipStudio asks TikTok for permission to:

  • Read basic profile information: the account's open ID, display name and avatar (user.info.basic).
  • Send videos to the account's inbox as drafts (video.upload). The owner reviews and posts them in the TikTok app; ClipStudio does not publish on TikTok by itself.

ClipStudio does not access email, contacts, messages, followers, viewing history, comments, analytics or any other account data.

How the data is used

  • Account names and pictures are shown in the app so the owner knows which account is connected.
  • Upload permissions are only used when the owner chooses to upload or send a specific clip.

Storage and sharing

  • Sign-in tokens and account information are stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • No data is sent to any server other than Google's/YouTube's and TikTok's own APIs.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Removing access

A connected account can be disconnected in the app's settings, which deletes its stored token from the computer. Access can also be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google/YouTube) or in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions (TikTok).

Third-party terms

ClipStudio uses YouTube API Services and the TikTok for Developers APIs. See the YouTube Terms of Service, the Google Privacy Policy and the TikTok Privacy Policy.
